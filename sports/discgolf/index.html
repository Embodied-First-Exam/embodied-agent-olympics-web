Rules

A 6-hole round (par 20) under PDGA rules through woods, two doglegs, out-of-bounds water and a mandatory, in a seeded wind. Each throw is a release (disc, speed, launch and nose angle, bank, spin, aim); the disc flies with a validated aerodynamic model.

PDGA Official Rules of Disc Golf (Professional Disc Golf Association, rev. 2026-01-01) 2026-01-015 declared deviations
