Rules

Singles on a small court under BWF laws: the court's lengths are scaled by 0.4, with the net at its BWF height of 1.524 m. Rally scoring, first to 5 points; the robots serve underhand into the diagonal court. The Franka Panda arm is held to the real Panda's limits on an x-y gantry (5 m/s, 50 m/s²). The shuttle has heavy drag (terminal speed 6.8 m/s) and does not bounce; the racket contact law has gain 4.

BWF Laws of Badminton (BWF Statutes, Section 4.1, version 5.0) 2025-04-269 declared deviations

Court scaling is one of 9 declared deviations from BWF laws.
