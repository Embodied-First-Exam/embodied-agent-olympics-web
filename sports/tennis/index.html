Rules

Singles tennis on a regulation court in real time, slowed down. A Franka Panda arm holds a racket on an x-y rail base; the ball has drag, Magnus lift, a hard-court bounce and a racket impact law. First to 5 points, with a lead of 2 from 4:4 and no score cap. Single matches are against a scripted opponent (levels 1–4); duels have two players. The players serve under ITF Rules 16–22, in tie-break order.

ITF Rules of Tennis 2026 (English) 20267 declared deviations

Matches still end after 400 racket hits or 40 points played, with the declared tie-break.
