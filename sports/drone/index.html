Rules

Fly a quadrotor through a seeded course of numbered gates under FAI F9U drone-racing rules, by its front camera and its own telemetry. Alone: three qualifying rounds; duel: heats on the same course in parallel worlds, first to two heat wins.

FAI Sporting Code Section 4 (Aeromodelling), Volume F9 Drone Sports, chapter C, class F9U Drone Racing 2026 edition (effective 1 January 2026)10 declared deviations
